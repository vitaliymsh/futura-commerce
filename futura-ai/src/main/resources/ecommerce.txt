# Futura Commerce Platform Knowledge Base & Operating Rules

## 1. Order & Fulfillment Policies
- Order Processing: Orders placed before 16:00 UTC are processed and dispatched on the same business day.
- Delivery Windows: Standard shipping takes 2-4 business days. Express shipping delivers within 24-48 hours.
- Tracking & Carriers: Real-time logistics tracking updates are published upon carrier manifest handoff (DHL, FedEx, UPS).

## 2. Returns, Refunds & Dispute Arbitration
- 30-Day Return Guarantee: Unopened and undamaged products can be returned within 30 days of confirmed delivery.
- Return Workflow:
  1. Customer files return request under Orders dashboard.
  2. Operator reviews ticket within 24 hours under After-Sales queue.
  3. Pre-paid shipping label is dispatched automatically upon approval.
  4. Refund is credited to original payment method within 3-5 business days upon warehouse receipt.
- Damaged / Defective Goods: Immediate replacement or instant full refund provided upon photo verification.

## 3. Product Catalog & Inventory Governance
- SKU Stock Thresholds: Low-stock warning is triggered when SKU available quantity falls below 10 units.
- Price Integrity: Promotional discounts (Coupons, Seckill flash sales, Full reductions) cannot exceed 70% of base MSRP.
- Category Taxonomy: Level 1 (Macro department), Level 2 (Product family), Level 3 (Specific subcategory).

## 4. Payment & Billing Rules
- Supported Gateways: Credit/Debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, SEPA / Wire transfer.
- Invoicing: Automated VAT/GST invoice generation is triggered upon order completion and available for download.
- Anti-Fraud Shield: Orders exceeding $5,000 or with mismatched billing/shipping addresses trigger secondary verification.
